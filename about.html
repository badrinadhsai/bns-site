BNS/A The short version

I’m a developer who reads the brief properly.

BNS is my independent practice: I design and build websites, search systems, AI helpers and automation — personally, start to finish. No team to brief, no layers to pay for, no dilution.

A/01

Why this studio exists.

Too many small businesses get one of two bad deals: an overpriced agency that hands the work to juniors, or a bargain freelancer who disappears mid-project. I built BNS as the third option — senior-level craft, direct contact, honest scoping.

My rule is simple: understand the business problem first, then choose the smallest technology that solves it properly. A website that earns enquiries beats an impressive one that doesn’t. Automation you understand beats AI you can’t explain.

If your problem genuinely needs something I don’t do, I’ll tell you that too — and point you in the right direction. That honesty is the whole business model.
